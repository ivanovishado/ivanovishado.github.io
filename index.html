04 / About

About

Ivan Galaviz is a Senior Software Engineer at Netflix, where he builds the agentic workflows for promotional assets power marketing campaigns for titles worldwide. His work lives where distributed systems meet human craft—and occasionally, it leaves the atmosphere.

Before Netflix he was a Senior Engineer at Nubank (Clojure) and an engineer at Wizeline (Go, Python), helping platforms process hundreds of billions of events. He studied computer engineering in Guadalajara, and in 2024 an experiment he helped build flew to the International Space Station aboard SpaceX CRS-31.

Ivan offers one-on-one software engineering mentorship in English and Spanish, covering system design, career growth, technical interviews, and moving from Latin America to global tech. Sessions are remote, last 45 minutes, and cost 500 MXN.
