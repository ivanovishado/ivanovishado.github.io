MCB-1 went to orbit.

A biomimetic material inspired by muscle fibres, built by Mat X Space, a collective of young Mexican scientists and engineers. It launched on SpaceX CRS-31 in November 2024 and was tested aboard the International Space Station through the MISSE platform, against the temperature swings of orbit.
