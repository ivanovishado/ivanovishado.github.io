Browsing and analytics

This website uses a self-hosted Umami instance to count page visits and referrers. Umami does not set cookies, does not collect personal identifiers, and does not track you across websites.

Cloudflare routes traffic to the site and processes standard network metadata such as IP addresses to deliver pages reliably and protect against abuse.

Mentorship sessions

When you book a mentorship session, scheduling and payment happen through Cal.com, and the call takes place over Google Meet. Those services handle your booking details under their own privacy policies.

Everything you share in a mentorship session, including resumes, career plans, interview targets, and architecture questions, stays confidential. Follow-up notes are shared only with you.

Email and data requests

If you email me at me@ivanovishado.dev, I use your address only to reply to your message or coordinate a session. You can ask to delete any emails or mentorship notes at any time by writing to the same address.
